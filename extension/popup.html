Album Archiver

Google Photos · v1.0.2

Archive the photos in an album. Photos stay in the album and are hidden from the main Photos feed.

1. Scan your album

Open an album in Google Photos, then open a photo inside it. The scan finds the first photo and records the album’s photo IDs.

2. Archive the scanned photos

A separate working tab opens. Keep it open while the extension checks each photo.
